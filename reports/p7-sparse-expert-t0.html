Sparse experts are small per worker. Cold fan-out is worse than full.

Four coordinator-routed expert workers reproduced centralized sparse gradients and AdamW byte-for-byte. Individual payload fell 63.36%, but duplicating the shared head made the cold colony round trip 50.15% larger than full replication.

Continue P7; change the shared-head contract first.

Test a frozen cached output head before T1/process scaling. Persistence—not sparsity alone—is what makes this topology economical.

Exact coordinator/worker decomposition

The coordinator computed the shared transformer trunk, capacity-bounded router, and router auxiliary loss. Each worker received only its routed hidden rows and targets plus one expert, final norm, and an untied output head. Workers computed complete token losses independently, then returned expert/head gradients and shared-trunk input adjoints. The coordinator accumulated experts in a fixed order, clipped globally, and retained AdamW authority.

The returned traffic includes 262,144 bytes of routed hidden input adjoints. The cached norm/output-head state is 2,098,176 bytes—79.90% of each cold worker payload—and its parameter gradients dominate result upload.

Routing was balanced by intervention

Unconstrained top-1 counts were [108, 147, 152, 105], a 16.87% load coefficient of variation. A capacity of 128 tokens per expert rerouted 44 of 512 tokens (8.59%) to produce [128, 128, 128, 128]. Perfect measured balance therefore does not show that the learned router naturally balanced load.

What this does—and does not—establish

Established

  • All four independent expert objectives map exactly.
  • Router, shared trunk, and every expert hold optimizer step 1.
  • Per-worker state is materially below full sparse state.
  • Expert affinity plus persistent shared cache can improve aggregate bytes.

Still open

  • The untied head deliberately increases shared model state.
  • T0 is random initialization, one step, and synthetic data.
  • No process/network RSS, latency, retry, convergence, or quality result exists yet.
  • Capacity rerouting can change specialization quality.

Next bounded P7 control

  1. Freeze and cache final norm/output head in both centralized and distributed sparse controls.
  2. Train router, shared trunk, and experts only; preserve exact accumulation and AdamW.
  3. Recompute cold/warm result traffic without duplicated head gradients.
  4. Only if aggregate economics improve, advance that contract to authenticated T1 and process workers.
Decision rule: persistence must produce a meaningful aggregate—not merely individual—advantage without breaking exactness. Otherwise this sparse arrangement is not suitable for opportunistic volunteer assignments.
