# Historical capability-report template

This filename is retained because the Record Patch prototype links to it. Its
mandatory benchmark thresholds and promotion labels belonged to the historical
`orcacolony_capability_research_v1` experiment and are not the framework for new
campaigns.

Use [`CAMPAIGN_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md`](CAMPAIGN_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md) for new work. It
requires the reporting agent to preserve campaign-owner-defined evaluation,
evidence, limitations, and contributor credit without choosing the campaign's
task, decision criteria, or next experiment.
